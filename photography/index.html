Photography Gallery

I picked up photography about eight years ago after finding my father’s old camera tucked away in a drawer. What started as curiosity quickly turned into a habit of looking closer at the world around me and preserving everyday moments before they pass.

I’m especially drawn to wildlife, nature, and motorsport photography, subjects that demand patience, timing, and a bit of stealth. Whether waiting quietly for an animal to move naturally or crouching behind a trackside barrier as motorcycles fly past, those moments are unpredictable, fast, and genuine.

Alongside real-world shoots, I also practice virtual photography inside video games. Game worlds are built with incredible artistic care, and framing shots inside them feels like a quiet collaboration with the environment and lighting artists who created those spaces. It’s another way to explore light, composition, and mood, just with a virtual lens instead of a physical one.
